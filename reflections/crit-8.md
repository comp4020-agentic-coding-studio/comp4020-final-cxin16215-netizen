# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Two decisions moved it forward. Both changed what I mean by being together.

The first was lingering company. I started with a strict rule: the garden only
grows while at least two people are there. Most visitors will probably arrive
alone, though, and I did not want their first view to be a grey, wilting garden.
Now someone who left a few minutes ago still counts for a while. Growth still
depends on company, but two people no longer have to overlap exactly.

The second was making every screen show the same garden. The prototype had its
own clock and random wild flowers, so two visitors would have seen different
things. I changed the season, wild flowers and planted flower shapes to follow
shared state, real time and each flower's id. That turned a picture running on
one screen into a place people can actually share.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I had been picturing the showcase, with a room full of people. Lingering
company made me think about the visitor who arrives on their own, which is
probably the more common case. I want to check that case earlier in future:
what does someone actually see when the ideal conditions are missing? A rule
can make sense in the design and still give that person a poor experience.
